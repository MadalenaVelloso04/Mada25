Concept

Peppa Please was a brief that sparked an instant interest in me from the very start. As the first project of the new year, 2026, I wanted to push myself beyond my limits. I aimed to go further and create, from scratch, not only striking branding but also an innovative product and packaging design. The brand’s tone of voice was defined as:

  • Bold
  • Spicy
  • Cheeky
  • Fun

The branding was developed hand in hand with the product, with both following the same core idea: no straight lines, visual messiness, but cleanliness in the user experience. The typographic choices were based on the bold and fun aspects of the tone of voice—off-centered, almost naïve and childlike—while remaining organized and thoughtfully placed at their core. The color palette followed a bold and spicy approach, using extremely saturated colors structured around a flavor-based logic. To reinforce the cheeky and fun character of the brand, I focused on storytelling through taglines and copy, always adding a playful twist. Overall, the visual system is built on the idea of having almost no rules and no straight lines; however, beneath this apparent chaos, there is a clear and intentional logic guiding every element.

The Packaging Design

The sauce bottle. Issues such as splashed sauce, dirty clothes, and poor sauce flow are almost dogmatic experiences. The user experience is based on being “used to it,” and I chose not to remain neutral about that factor. Therefore, I developed a completely new mechanism. The bottle adopts a parallelepiped format: easy to hold, almost hand-sized, and ergonomically comfortable. The opening from which the sauce flows is neither straight nor too thin; it allows enough space for a smooth flow, eliminating any chance of “exploding” due to internal vacuum tension. The opening also features a protective sliding barrier that is easy to push back, providing additional hand stability by creating balanced and soft pressure throughout use. To complete the design and ensure cleanliness and protection—no more dried sauce on the opening—the bottle is topped with a lid roughly the same size as the body of the product. The lid has a soft, glossy texture, reinforcing both hygiene and the overall tactile experience.
