The Creative Process

This project is the first of many in this area, as I developed a genuine interest for this specific industry. The main goal was to create three covers, one album and two singles, using the same motif.

As you can see, the topic has its main focus on this particular flower, the lily. Its simbolism revolves around peace, purity and rebirth. Tim Bernardes, the author of the albums that I'm designing, is a brazilian singer and songwriter with a distinguished talent of writing poetic pieces that have a sense of nostalgy, pain, sorrow, but also, restart.

"Recomeçar" for example, means literally restart.
