Concept
Overview
—
001

A Botanical Experience

Petali is a botanical soda brand that celebrates fresh, organic, and fruity sensations. Light, sparkling, and enchanting, each sip is infused with delicate floral notes and crafted from natural ingredients, creating a magical and refreshing experience.

Petali started as a dream concept for me—magical, fun, floral, and beautiful. From the very beginning, I wanted its identity to reflect exactly that feeling. Being a soda, the instinctive approach might have been metallic, modern, and bold, but I chose the opposite. This deliberate choice creates a more exclusive and unique experience, setting Petali apart from the ordinary.

Process
Development
—
002

From Research to Magic

I began by researching health-promoting flowers, gathering a wealth of information and details. Fast forward, I paired each flower with a complementary, flavorful fruit. This initial research laid the foundation for exploring visual interpretations. I followed an exclusive creative direction: soft, blurred, and dynamic patterns inspired by real flowers.

The intricate serifs, delicate floral patterns, and subtle water droplets are key elements in every design. The logo became the centerpiece of Petali's magical identity, featuring delicate sparkles and the elegant serif of the font Erotica. While each can reflects a different flavor and flower, the logo remains consistent, providing a unifying and recognizable element across the range.

Touchpoints

003

Bringing the Brand to Life

The identity extends across multiple touchpoints—from packaging and print materials to environmental graphics. Each application maintains the soft, magical aesthetic while adapting to its specific context.

The result is a cohesive system that feels enchanting and premium, reinforcing Petali's position as a unique botanical soda brand.
