The Creative Process

This project is the first of many in this area, as I developed a strong interest in illustration during my last semester of college. O Rapaz e o Mar is a children's book with a very specific goal: to slow down and be patient.

As I introduced, this book is a reflection of what I believe kids need in this day and age: slow dopamine. We are living in a challenging time where children are constantly overstimulated and triggered. Even in traditional formats, such as books, there's a tendency to create fast-paced, quickly resolved, and loud compositions and stories. That is exactly what I decided to move away from.

O Rapaz e o Mar tells the story of a boy and his sea, their cyclical connection and harmony, how nature embraces humans, and how humans should also embrace nature and its natural movement. The goal is to calm children's minds and let them imagine what it would be like if they were like this little boy, with so much curiosity, compassion, and love.
