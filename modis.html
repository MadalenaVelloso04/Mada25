Concept
Overview
—
001

An Eye for Fashion

Modis is a modern, editorial, and avant-garde fashion academy built on the concept of "an eye for fashion" — where vision meets creativity. The identity expresses this through the logotype, where the O becomes the eye itself, symbolizing vision and perspective.

The logo combines two O's with a small head to form the silhouette of a figure in a flowing dress, creating a unique visual language that speaks to both observation and creation.

Process
Development
—
002

From Sketch to Identity

The project began with hand-drawn sketches, exploring different ways to capture the essence of vision and fashion through shapes and symbols. From these ideas, I refined the concept digitally, experimenting with proportions and compositions.

I refined the logo in Adobe Illustrator, focusing on clean lines and adaptability. With Photoshop, I created editorial-style mockups to test textures and contexts, ensuring a modern, dynamic feel.

Applications
Touchpoints
—
003

Bringing the Brand to Life

The identity system extends across print and digital touchpoints, from stationery and packaging to website animations and environmental graphics. Each application maintains the core visual language while adapting to its specific context.

The result is a cohesive, recognizable system that feels fresh and contemporary across all brand expressions.
